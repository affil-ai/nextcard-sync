✓ Installed

Start with your
first card offers

Open the extension, then follow these three steps.

Or click Chrome’s puzzle-piece icon, then nextcard sync.

Pin nextcard sync in the puzzle-piece menu for easy access.

  1. 1

    Sign in to nextcard

    In the extension, click Sign in to nextcard. Use the same account as the website, then return to the extension.

  2. 2

    Check your card offers

    Open Offers, choose your issuer, and click Check for offers. Sign in on the issuer’s website and keep that tab open.

  3. 3

    Review and activate

    Return to the extension. Choose your card, review the offer count, then confirm activation.

    This activates the available offers for that card, up to your plan’s limit. Checking alone does not activate them.

Once saving finishes, view your offers in nextcard ↗.

Reopen this page from Setup guide in the extension.
